Privacy Policy

How Kingdom Fellowship Christian Church collects, uses, protects and retains information when you use this website and its online ministry features.

Privacy matters. This policy is written for the current website and should be reviewed and approved by the Church before being adopted as its final legal privacy notice.

Last updated: 29 August 2026

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the website may receive your name, contact details, messages, comments, registrations, giving-related information, administrator account information and technical information such as IP address, browser type and security logs.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide church services and programmes, moderate public comments, operate livestream and media features, administer authorized accounts, maintain security, prevent abuse and improve the website.

3. Public Comments and Chat

Messages submitted to public chat or comments may be visible to other visitors when the relevant feature is enabled. Do not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information in public chat.

4. Contact Information

Church contact information may be displayed publicly so visitors can reach the Church. Public contact details can be collected by automated systems, so visitors should avoid sending sensitive personal information through ordinary email or public forms.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

The website may use essential cookies or browser storage to support security, administrator sessions and website functionality. The administrator session cookie is intended to be protected with Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite controls when the site is operating over HTTPS. Non-essential analytics or advertising cookies should not be introduced without updating this policy and any required consent mechanism.

6. Livestreams and Third-Party Services

Video, audio, maps, fonts, storage and other services may be provided by third parties. When these services are loaded, those providers may receive technical information according to their own policies. The Church should review and list the third-party services actually enabled on the production site.

7. Media and Uploaded Content

Authorized administrators may upload church media. Uploads are subject to file-type, size and security controls. The Church should retain only media and personal information that it has a legitimate reason to keep.

8. Administrative Accounts

Administrator information is used to authenticate and authorize access to the administration system. Administrative actions may be logged for security, auditing and troubleshooting. Administrator credentials must not be shared.

9. Security

The website uses measures such as HTTPS, secure session cookies, security headers, access controls, input validation, rate limiting and upload validation. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so suspicious activity should be reported promptly to the Church.

10. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal obligations, security, accounting, safeguarding or legitimate church administration.

11. Your Requests

Subject to applicable law and the Church's legal obligations, you may ask the Church what personal information it holds about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask about deletion or restriction where appropriate.

12. Children

Parents and guardians should guide children when using interactive website features. The Church should not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children through public chat, forms or other features.

13. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when website features, data practices, legal requirements or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official contact channel published on the Church website. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information in an initial privacy enquiry.
